How it works

← Back to app

A caseworker's job in this tool is five steps: upload evidence, check the page previews, let the vision model scan each page, review every row against the source image, then export the filled spreadsheet and claim breakdown. Nothing is trusted blind — every scanned value sits next to the page it came from so you can verify it before it's used.

Upload → Preview → Process (vision scan) → Review → Export

1 Upload evidence

Start on the main form. Set the worker's weekly rest day and standard break, and optionally a half-day of week (for a 5.5-day week — see Step 4 on how this feeds overtime) — these feed the guardrail checks later. Work week (5.5 or 6 days) is no longer set here: it's read straight off the IPA once scanned, editable in the IPA review card if it needs correcting. If anything about these specific pages could otherwise be misread (e.g. "8 - 9pm on every date actually means 8am to 9pm"), say so in "Reading notes for this scan" — it's sent to the scan from the very first attempt. Then attach the IPA letter (required) and timesheet files (required unless a payslip already states overtime hours). Bank statements, medical certificates, medical bills, and payslips are optional cross-checks.

2 Preview before scanning

Once files are chosen, each page gets a thumbnail with a rotate button and a remove (✕) button. Check every page is upright here — a sideways scan silently misreads dates and times downstream, so this is the one manual check the app can't do for you.

3 Process — the vision scan

Clicking Extract details kicks off a background job. Each timesheet page is read 3 independent times and reconciled (unanimity / majority) before being trusted; disagreement escalates to a fallback model rather than being silently guessed. This step runs behind the thumbnails you just confirmed and can take 15–20+ minutes for a full batch — the page polls and streams results in as each one finishes.

4 Review — every row against its source

This is the step that matters most. Each date's start/end/break sits right beside the page it was read from. Rows highlighted yellow were flagged by the scan itself (a guess, a multi-shift day, an implausible reading, hours over the 12h/day statutory cap) — worth a closer look before trusting them. Clicking into a row highlights the matching region on the source image. Nothing is written to the spreadsheet until you click Generate; any row you edit here is saved as a raw-vs-corrected pair to improve future scans.

Overtime's "normal hours before OT kicks in" depends on the declared work week: 8h on a 5.5-day week's normal days, a flat 4h (no lunch) on that week's declared half-day, or 44/6 (≈7.33h) evenly across every day of a 6-day week.

A genuinely blank box (not a rest day, not an MC) still gets filled in as a worked day using the declared default shift timing — flagged "Auto-inserted", since there's no real evidence for it. That default-shift guess is credited as a standard day's pay but never as overtime, no matter how long the configured default shift is — OT is only ever paid against an actual observed clock-out time.

5 Optional claims

Below the main upload fields, four optional toggles cover entitlements the IPA/timesheet scan alone doesn't compute: salary-in-lieu of termination notice, salary-in-lieu of unused annual leave, sick leave pay, and a non-fixed allowance — a contract-based pay rule the IPA never states (e.g. a late-work top-up, a Sunday meal allowance, a one-time housing payment).

The non-fixed allowance is written as one free paragraph, in plain English, any way you like — e.g. "Add $5 daily if clocked out past 5pm. Between Sep 1-10 2026, the company provided $250 one-time for housing." Nothing is parsed live as you type; clicking Extract details sends the whole paragraph to be read once, turning each sentence into either a recurring per-day rule or a one-time lump sum (correctly paid once, not multiplied across every day of a date range), and a summary shows what was understood so you can check it before trusting the total.

6 Export

Scroll to the bottom for the computed Overtime / Rest Day / Public Holiday / Non-Fixed Allowance breakdown, totalled month by month. From here, generate a Word or PDF version of the Worker Personal Details claim breakdown for printing.

7 Scan history

Every scan — timesheet, bank statement, medical bills, payslips — is logged with its cost. Click View on any row to reopen that scan's full review screen later, e.g. to re-check a case or re-export after a correction.
