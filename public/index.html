Timesheet → Wage Claim Calculator

Upload one worker's timesheets — PDFs or photos (jpg/png), one or more months. Each page is read by Qwen, then you review and correct the detected hours before generating the calculation.xltx spreadsheet.
Handwritten years are often misread — the model only reads day/month off the page, this dropdown decides the year (and which sheet tab is used).

    Page-by-page review

    Every date's Start/End/Lunch is editable right beside its source page — fix anything the scan got wrong, remove a row that shouldn't be there, or add a missing day below. Rows highlighted yellow were flagged by the scan (a guess, a multi-shift day, or an implausible reading) and are worth a closer look. Nothing is written to the spreadsheet until you click Generate.
