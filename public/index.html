Leave the override blank to use the statutory tier (Employment Act ss.10-11) based on length of service. Fill it in only if the employment contract itself specifies a different notice period.
  • Less than 26 weeks' service — 1 day's notice
  • 26 weeks to less than 2 years' service — 1 week's notice
  • 2 years to less than 5 years' service — 2 weeks' notice
  • 5 years or more service — 4 weeks' notice

Unpaid non-hospitalization sick days
Unpaid hospitalization sick days
Gross rate of pay = basic salary + monthly allowances (Employment Act s.89).
  • Not payable before 3 months' service
  • 3 months' service — 5 non-hospitalization / 15 hospitalization days
  • 4 months' service — 8 non-hospitalization / 30 hospitalization days
  • 5 months' service — 11 non-hospitalization / 45 hospitalization days
  • 6+ months' service — 14 non-hospitalization / 60 hospitalization days (the hospitalization cap already includes the non-hospitalization days within it)
  • "Unpaid" days are a caseworker judgment call — cross-check the worker's medical certificates against the Basic Salary Breakdown table for that month to see whether pay was actually docked

Bank statement (optional — PDF or photo)

A worker sometimes says they were paid "some" but not all of what's owed — this is a separate check against their own bank statement, not tied to the claim scan above and not written to the spreadsheet. Every incoming (credit) transaction on the statement is extracted — nothing is filtered by keyword — so delete whatever's irrelevant in the review below before comparing against the claim total.

Medical bills (optional — PDF or photo)

An employer of a Work Permit holder is generally required to bear the worker's medical costs — this checks whether that actually happened. Every medical bill/invoice is scanned for its billed date and amount. Review the extracted amounts below, mark whether each one was reimbursed by the employer, and the app totals the unreimbursed amount for you.

    Page-by-page review

    Every date's Start/End/Break is editable right beside its source page — fix anything the scan got wrong, remove a row that shouldn't be there, or add a missing day below. Rows highlighted yellow were flagged by the scan (a guess, a multi-shift day, or an implausible reading) and are worth a closer look. Nothing is written to the spreadsheet until you click Generate. Any page you edit is automatically saved (original scan + your correction) when you click Generate — this is what helps improve future scans.
