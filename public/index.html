Half-day (5.5-day week only)

Only shown once the IPA's declared working days/week (editable in the IPA review card once scanned) comes out to 5.5 — a 6-day week has no shorter half-day, every day gets the same 44/6h threshold instead. On the half-day, overtime starts right after the 4th worked hour (no lunch break built into its normal hours), not the usual 8-hour mark. Reading notes for this scan (optional)

Tell the scan about anything on these specific pages that could otherwise be misread — e.g. "8 - 9pm on every date actually means 8am to 9pm", "the Qty column is clock-out time, not a quantity", or "ignore the printed total column, it's wrong". Applies to every page in this submission.

IPA letter (PDF or photo, optional)

Employer/salary/OT-rate terms are read off it alongside the timesheets, in the same submit. Skip this if you'd rather fill in worker name, FIN, employer, salary, etc. by hand — the fields stay editable below either way, and claims recompute as you fill them in.

Timesheet files (optional if a payslip covers overtime — PDF or photo)

Upload the worker's daily timesheets here — this is the main source for day-by-day hours, rest days worked, and public holidays worked. Skip this only if a payslip already states the month's overtime hours and you don't need day-level detail.

Bank statement (optional — PDF or photo)

Checks what was actually paid against the claim total — a separate check, not written to the spreadsheet. All incoming transactions get extracted; delete irrelevant ones before comparing.

Medical certificates (optional — PDF or photo)

Backs up timesheet "MC" days — a day without a matching certificate here gets flagged. Separate from medical bills below (MCs rarely show a billed amount).

Medical bills (optional — PDF or photo)

Employers must bear a Work Permit holder's medical costs — this checks if that happened. Mark each bill reimbursed or not; the app totals what's still owed.

Payslip (optional — PDF or photo)

Use when no day-by-day overtime from a timesheet — a payslip states the employer's own OT figure instead. Pick timesheet vs payslip per month once both exist.
